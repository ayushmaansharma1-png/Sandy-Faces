# Sandy-Faces
Campfire Song with Sand and people's faces



I have known for long, what this world holds....
Yet you stay so calm and naive, how do i tell you, my love.

On our horses we stroll, down the sandy shore.
I have an order to follow, but I'll see you again someday soon.

Bye, bye, bye , bye, hope it's not a goodbye.
I strum on my guitar, I think of better times.
Where all I have to worry about is whether or not I get a dime.

Thousand miles away from the city, I'll be back home someday.

The shores of this beach look so nice, I have never felt such breeze before.
On this campfire by the sandy shore, I reminisce about a familiar face, I'll be back before winter, to you , that I promise.
